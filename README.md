### 📖 My Story

💼 **Where I come from:** Financial markets professional with 7 years across P&L reconciliation, risk, and capital-markets operations — now combining that domain depth with Python, SQL, and machine learning to automate financial workflows and turn complex data into decisions.

🔭 **Where I am now:** I realized the most valuable part of my financial work was the automated data pipelines I built to solve those problems. I have since transitioned into machine learning, applying my domain expertise to build production-grade forecasting, trading, and risk systems.

🛠️ **What I do:**
* Financial analysis & reporting (P&L, reconciliation, variance breakdown, break resolution)
* Data engineering & automation (Knime ETL, SAP BI4 queries, Power Query — replacing manual work over 2M+ row datasets)
* Machine learning (time-series forecasting, classification, fraud/anomaly detection, risk scoring)
* MLOps & deployment (Docker, AWS, GitHub Actions, MLflow)
* Explainable AI (SHAP) for high-stakes financial decisions

🌱 **What I am learning right now:**
* Building end-to-end financial pipelines, dashboards, and ML engines that turn raw market/business data into decision-ready tools.
* Expanding my MLOps toolkit (Docker, GitHub Actions, AWS) to ensure models are scalable and production-ready.
